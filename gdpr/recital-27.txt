GDPR — Recital 27
Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation) | CELEX:32016R0679 | OJ L 119, 4.5.2016, p. 1
Source: EUR-Lex (Cellar XHTML, eli-subdivision id="rct_27"); fetched 2026-10-04

(27) This Regulation does not apply to the personal data of deceased persons. Member States may provide for rules regarding the processing of personal data of deceased persons.
